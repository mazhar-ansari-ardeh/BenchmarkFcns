.. rubric:: Mathematical Definition

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{aligned}
f_{hf}(x_1, x_2) &= 4x_1^2 + x_2^3 + x_1x_2 \\
g_{hf}(x_1, x_2) &= \frac{1}{x_1} + \frac{1}{x_2} - 2 \le 0 \\
f_{lf}(x_1, x_2) &= 4(x_1 + 0.1)^2 + (x_2 - 0.1)^3 + x_1x_2 + 0.1 \\
g_{lf}(x_1, x_2) &= \frac{1}{x_1} + \frac{1}{x_2 + 0.1} - 2.001 \le 0
\end{aligned}
