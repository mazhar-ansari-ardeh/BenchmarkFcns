.. rubric:: Mathematical Definition

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   f_1 = x_1^{0.2} + \frac{2}{|J_1|} \sum_{j \in J_1} (x_j - \sin(6\pi x_1 + \frac{j\pi}{n}))^2 \\
f_2 = 1 - x_1^{0.2} + \frac{2}{|J_2|} \sum_{j \in J_2} (x_j - \sin(6\pi x_1 + \frac{j\pi}{n}))^2 \\
J_1 = \{j | j \text{ is odd and } 2 \le j \le n\}, J_2 = \{j | j \text{ is even and } 2 \le j \le n\}
