.. rubric:: Mathematical Definition

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   \begin{aligned}
f_1(\textbf{x}) &= x_1 + \frac{2}{|J_1|} \sum_{j \in J_1} [x_j - \sin(6\pi x_1 + \frac{j\pi}{n})]^2 \\
f_2(\textbf{x}) &= 1 - x_1 + \frac{2}{|J_2|} \sum_{j \in J_2} [x_j - \sin(6\pi x_1 + \frac{j\pi}{n})]^2 \\
\text{Subject to: } & x_2 - \sin(6\pi x_1 + \frac{2\pi}{n}) - 0.5x_1 + 0.25 \ge 0 \\
& x_1 \in [0, 1], x_j \in [-2, 2]
\end{aligned}
