.. rubric:: Mathematical Definition

.. math::
   :nowrap:

   f(x_1, x_2, x_3) = 100 \left[ (x_3 - 10\theta(x_1, x_2))^2 + (\sqrt{x_1^2 + x_2^2} - 1)^2 \right] + x_3^2
\text{where } \theta(x_1, x_2) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \operatorname{atan2}(x_2, x_1)
