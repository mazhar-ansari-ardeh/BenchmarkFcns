.. rubric:: Mathematical Definition

.. math::

   \begin{aligned}
f_i(\textbf{x}) &= x_i, \quad i=1, \dots, M-1 \\
f_M(\textbf{x}) &= (1 + g(\textbf{x}_M)) [ M - \sum_{i=1}^{M-1} \frac{x_i}{1+g} (1 + \sin(3\pi x_i)) ] \\
g(\textbf{x}_M) &= 1 + \frac{9}{|x_M|} \sum_{x_j \in \textbf{x}_M} x_j
\end{aligned}
